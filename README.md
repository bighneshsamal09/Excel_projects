# Ferns and Petals (FNP) Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data from **Ferns and Petals (FNP)**, a
gifting company that sells products for occasions such as Diwali, Raksha
Bandhan, Holi, Valentine's Day, Birthdays, and Anniversaries.

The goal of the project is to create an interactive sales dashboard and
identify useful business insights related to **revenue, orders, delivery
time, customer spending, products, occasions, and cities**.

The project is based on the provided FNP Sales Analysis problem
statement and dashboard.

## Business Questions

The analysis answers the following key business questions:

1.  What is the total revenue?
2.  What is the average order-to-delivery time?
3.  How does sales performance fluctuate across the months of 2023?
4.  Which products generate the highest revenue?
5.  How much do customers spend on average?
6.  What is the sales performance of the top 5 products?
7.  Which are the top 10 cities by number of orders?
8.  Does higher order quantity affect delivery time?
9.  How does revenue compare across different occasions?
10. Which products are most popular for specific occasions?

## Dataset

The project uses three CSV datasets:

-   `customers.csv` --- customer-related information
-   `orders.csv` --- order, date, quantity, revenue, occasion, and
    delivery-related information
-   `products.csv` --- product information

These datasets are used together to perform the sales analysis.

## Dashboard

The dashboard contains the following visualizations and KPIs:

-   Total Orders
-   Total Revenue
-   Average Order-Delivery Time
-   Average Customer Spend
-   Revenue by Occasions
-   Revenue by Category
-   Revenue by Hour (Order Time)
-   Revenue by Months
-   Top 5 Products by Revenue
-   Top 10 Cities by Orders
-   Interactive filters for Order Date, Delivery Date, and Occasion

## Project Workflow

1.  Import the CSV datasets into Excel.
2.  Review and clean the data.
3.  Prepare the required fields for analysis.
4.  Create relationships/lookup logic between customers, orders, and
    products where required.
5.  Create PivotTables for the business questions.
6.  Create PivotCharts and KPI cards.
7.  Add slicers/timeline filters for interactive analysis.
8.  Build the final sales dashboard.
9.  Analyze the dashboard to identify business insights.

## Example Insights

The dashboard can be used to identify:

-   Overall revenue and order volume.
-   Months with stronger or weaker sales performance.
-   Highest-revenue product categories and products.
-   Occasions that contribute more revenue.
-   Cities generating the highest number of orders.
-   Customer average spending.
-   Order-to-delivery performance.
-   Hourly patterns in order revenue.

## Project Objective

The main objective is to use sales data to help FNP:

-   Improve sales strategy.
-   Understand customer purchasing behavior.
-   Identify high-performing products and occasions.
-   Identify important cities and markets.
-   Monitor delivery performance.
-   Improve customer satisfaction.
